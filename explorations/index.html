direction index

Each direction is one self-contained file: WebGL, no dependencies, no generated imagery. Every surface is procedural, and every quality is taken from a taste-library item as a technique rather than as a mood. Open one full-screen and move the pointer; each carries a single interaction that only makes sense for its own family. Wordmark and copy are placeholders throughout, and empty slots are drawn as empty.

Current set · material implemented

  • v6 Pixel Ordered dither on a 4×4 Bayer matrix through seven indexed colours. Cells are anchored in screen space so the field pops through them cell by cell instead of sliding. Edges left aliased. #E06A1F
  • v7 Screened field Orthogonal dot screen, never rotated to a rosette. Dot diameter tracks luminance alone; the chromatic fringe lives only in the falloff. The screen is placed, not applied edge to edge. #B5502A
  • v8 Instrument Bone-plastic fascia in unequal panels meeting at hard seams. Chamfered rather than radiused, visible fasteners, a clear shell over a forest chassis. The selector detents; it never eases. #E2600F
  • v9 Notation Two inks: near-black draws what exists, red asserts something about it. An isometric cube plots itself edge by edge over a registration lattice. Emptiness is the dominant surface. #C8322A
  • v10 Substrate Even illumination, no vignette anywhere. Fibre at two scales, flecks, foxing toward the margins, and a lime skin fallen away to rust render that stains outward across the break. #A85D3C
