direction index

Each direction is one self-contained file: WebGL, no dependencies, no generated imagery. Every surface is procedural, and every quality is taken from a taste-library item as a technique rather than as a mood. Wordmark and copy are placeholders throughout, and empty slots are drawn as empty.

The substrate set is five readings of a single family, on one page architecture and one pair of typefaces, so the material is the only variable. The family set takes one direction from each of five families and varies everything; each of those carries a single interaction that only makes sense for its own family.

Rebuild set · five aesthetics, one corrected structure

  • r1 Cinematic Dark warm ground, filmic light. Hero runs the supplied Dithering shader — warp, 4×4, #EC4E02 at 30% screen, faster on hover. #EC4E02
  • r1b Cinematic · landscape r1 with a different hero: a Perlin terrain generated at load and flown over, heightfield-raycast and dithered. New landscape every visit; ?seed=N reproduces one. #E4863A
  • r2 Measured Two inks on a cool paper. Hairline isometric construction with one red dimension; work set as a ruled index rather than media. #B02A20
  • r3 Panel Bone fascia, forest inlay, one functional orange. Hard seams and chamfers throughout; work in a panel grid, nothing rounded. #D6621A
  • r4 Screened Ink ground with a placed dot screen, never edge to edge. Editorial scale, work stacked full width. #E8A33C
  • r5 Ledger Warm light ground with an index that stays with you as the record scrolls. A survey contour, one datum picked out. #126E5C

Substrate set · five readings of one family

  • s1 Kraft Uncoated kraft, pulp colour running all the way through. A mid-tone paper rather than a pale one tinted warm, so the ink has less room and every value sits closer to its neighbours. #7A3B22
  • s2 Fragments Torn papers, same family and visibly not the same batch. Edges ragged at two scales with the pale fibre lip exposed. Depth from overlap order and one step of value only. #8A5A2B
  • s3 Granite Crystalline break at crystal pitch, not terrazzo pitch. Every piece shows both a fresh fractured face and a weathered outer one. The only substrate here with no warmth in it. #7C4A3A
  • s4 Linen Plain weave, warp over weft and back, with slub along every thread. Crowns run warm because they face out, valleys cool because they sit in their own shade. Crush blooms paler. #5C6842
  • s5 Plaster Lime skin and rust render level in one plane, neither behind the other. A hard break joins them one way and a soft stain bleeds back across it the other. #8F4F30

Family set · one direction each

  • v6 Pixel Ordered dither on a 4×4 Bayer matrix through seven indexed colours. Cells are anchored in screen space so the field pops through them cell by cell instead of sliding. Edges left aliased. #E06A1F
  • v7 Screened field Orthogonal dot screen, never rotated to a rosette. Dot diameter tracks luminance alone; the chromatic fringe lives only in the falloff. The screen is placed, not applied edge to edge. #B5502A
  • v8 Instrument Bone-plastic fascia in unequal panels meeting at hard seams. Chamfered rather than radiused, visible fasteners, a clear shell over a forest chassis. The selector detents; it never eases. #E2600F
  • v9 Notation Two inks: near-black draws what exists, red asserts something about it. An isometric cube plots itself edge by edge over a registration lattice. Emptiness is the dominant surface. #C8322A
  • v10 Substrate Even illumination, no vignette anywhere. Fibre at two scales, flecks, foxing toward the margins, and a lime skin fallen away to rust render that stains outward across the break. #A85D3C
