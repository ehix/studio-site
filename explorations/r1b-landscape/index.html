What I make

Websites

Marketing sites, portfolios and small applications. Designed and built end to end, on infrastructure you could hand to somebody else later without an apology attached.

Games

Small games and interactive pieces, built with the same attention to how a thing feels under the hand as to how it looks on the page.

Publications

Print and digital. Type set properly, images prepared properly, and files a printer will accept without needing to telephone you about them.

How it goes

One conversation

You describe the problem. I tell you whether I am the right person to solve it, and roughly what it costs. If I am not, I say so.

One person

The same pair of hands from the first sketch to the deploy. Nothing gets handed over, and so nothing gets lost in the handing.

One thing at a time

Few projects at once, so yours is the one being worked on rather than the one queued behind somebody else’s.

Tell me what you are trying to build.

I will tell you honestly whether I am the right person to make it, and what it would take.
