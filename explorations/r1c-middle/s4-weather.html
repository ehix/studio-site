What this is

Nothing new appears on the page. There was already wind in the grass and haze in the hero, and they knew nothing about each other — two noise fields at similar frequencies, which reads as two weathers in one place. This makes them one.

A single clock and a single gust field, sampled at a depth: the hero at the top of the descent, the grass at the bottom. A gust arrives everywhere, a little later further down. Watch the trace in the corner, then watch the same shape arrive in the grass at the end of the page.

It drives TERRAIN.params and GRASS.params — the same knobs the tuning panel turns, sixty times a second. No file is patched and nothing is replaced, which is why the panel still works while the weather is running. Open it and watch the sliders move.
