What this is

No new graphic at all. A hairline in the gutter carries a height that falls as you read — 1,240m over the range in the hero, 8cm in the grass at the sign-off. It does not illustrate the descent, it states it.

The number is anchored to elements, not to scroll percentage: add a case study and every altitude still reads correctly. And it falls logarithmically, because four and a half orders of magnitude done linearly sits above 1,000 for half the page and then drops off a cliff.

Watch the station names in the rail — range, hillside, field, path, tussock, stem, blade. That ladder is the whole idea, written down.
