What this is

The middle of the page is a survey of the terrain the hero is flying over. Not terrain-like imagery — the same heightmap, read straight out of TERRAIN.world and drawn two ways: in plan as a contour map, and in section as an elevation profile cut along the flight line.

The crosshair on the plan is the camera. It is where you actually are. Watch it for a minute and it moves, because the hero is still flying.

The argument for it: a contour map of this terrain cannot also be a coffee roaster's, which was the exact charge against the four light fields it replaces. It is also nearly free — the 885,000 Perlin evaluations were already paid for by the hero at load.

The site you are reading is the same argument as the work.
