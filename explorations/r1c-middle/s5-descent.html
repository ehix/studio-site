What this is — and the argument against it

The purest form of the idea, and the one I said to hold back. Here it is anyway, so the argument can be had against something real.

It is not a camera. It is four silhouette generators (ridge, hillside, tussock, stem) cross-faded on scroll depth, two always live at once. A truly continuous zoom would cycle noise octaves, which is the right answer when you are magnifying one material. These four scales are not one material: a ridgeline and a grass blade do not differ by frequency, they differ by what they are.

The three costs, plainly. It sits behind every word on the page, so it has to live near the floor of the ramp and under a scrim. Notice that the body copy here needs slabs to stay readable, which the other four showcases do not. It renders on scroll, so it is cheap when still and the most expensive thing on the page when thrown. And it is one more full-viewport canvas on a page whose hero already costs 14ms a frame.
