Stock
Off-white linen #D3D6C6, drifting between grey-green and a paler bloom. Colour is not flat anywhere on it.
Weave
Plain weave, warp over weft and back. The only substrate here with a direction in it.
Temperature
Thread crowns run warm because they face outward; the shadows in the weave carry a faint cool cast. Render both the same and the cloth becomes a hatch pattern.
Crush
Where the surface has been crushed and creased it blooms paler, and the iridescent shift toward blue shows at those edges.
Pigment
#5C6842 at 4.04:1, so it carries areas and large type but never small text. The lip on it reaches 5.11:1.
Type
Young Serif 400 and Courier Prime 400/700, both SIL OFL 1.1. Licence files are not yet vendored alongside them.
Measured
Ink on stock 9.95:1 · quiet 5.39:1 · lip on pigment 5.11:1
