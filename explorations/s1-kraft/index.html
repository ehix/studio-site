Stock
Uncoated kraft #AE9068. A mid-tone paper, not a pale one tinted warm — the pulp colour runs all the way through.
Light
Even throughout. No vignette, no hotspot, no directional drama anywhere on the sheet.
Depth
Read from overlap order and one step of value. Nothing casts a shadow.
Range
Every value within a step or two of its neighbours. Widen it and this becomes a composition about contrast rather than a material.
